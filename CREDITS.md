## Preview stock photography — hero, about, rooms, gallery

All photographs in `/public/images/stock/` are **stock images placed as demo
placeholders** (user instruction, 29 September 2026: "Place stock photos in
photo holder"). They are **NOT photographs of Ha La Tyngkong Stay Inn** and
must never be presented as such. The site labels them honestly:

- The hero carries a permanent "Preview demo · placeholder photography" chip.
- The gallery section carries a "Preview imagery" demo note.
- Room detail dialogs carry a "Preview image" badge.
- Every image `alt` text begins with "Preview placeholder —".

**Before public go-live: replace all of these with the owner's own photos of
the property, and delete this stock set.** The Google listing exposes 236
